1. **Add 6 string operations to _FILTER_OPS** (sendMail.py, line 1029)
2. **Implement operations in _eval_string()** (sendMail.py, line 1106)
3. **Import re module** for regex support
4. **Add unit + integration tests**

## Scope

Supported operations should include ALL defined in sendMail.py:

**Numeric**: gt, lt, ge, le, greater than, less than, greater or equal to, less or equal to  
**Equality**: is, is not, is equal to, is not equal to, eq, ne  
**List**: in, not in, one of, not one of  
**Text**: contains, does not contain, starts with, ends with, matches, does not match  
**Empty**: is empty, is not empty  
**Email**: is bounced, is not bounced (likely mail-specific, lower priority)
